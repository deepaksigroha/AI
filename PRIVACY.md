# Privacy Policy – ImageClean Pro

*Developer: Deepak Sigroha · Effective: November 2026*

ImageClean Pro processes images **only on your device**.

- **No network access.** The app does not request the Android `INTERNET` permission and therefore cannot upload images or data.
- **No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.**
- **Images** you select are read only to display and analyse them. The app never modifies the original file; exported copies are saved to `Pictures/ImageClean Pro` (Android 10+) or to the app's own folder (Android 9).
- **Location data** found in photos is displayed so you can decide whether to remove it. It is never collected or transmitted.
- **History and reports** are stored in the app's private database, excluded from cloud backup and device transfer, and can be deleted in *Settings → Clear history*. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Certificates** you import for C2PA verification stay in the app's private storage.
- **Permissions:** `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION` (to show you GPS tags in photos you open) and, on Android 9 only, legacy storage write access.

Questions: contact the developer, Deepak Sigroha, via the project repository.
